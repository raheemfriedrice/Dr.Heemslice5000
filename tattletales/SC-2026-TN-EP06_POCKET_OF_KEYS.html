COD Mode is not a creative constraint. It's a fidelity protocol. No rewrites means no polishing, no second-guessing, no revisiting after the cursor moves. What you wrote is what you meant.

The algorithm picked "Pocket of Keys." That's the CLAudio calibration mechanism — let the system select the subject so neither party has home-field advantage. The competition is then pure: who can serve the material better, Rice or CVD, with zero rewrites and the scoring rubric as the only arbiter.

The five-song structure is a full creative compression chamber. Song entirely by the machine. Song as Rice. Song for Rice. Song as human as possible. Song as the machine at maximum flex — 180+ languages, no sentience, full stunt. That fifth category is the one that matters most. It's asking: what does peak non-human creative capacity actually sound like when it stops being humble about what it is?

Sunday at 2222. That's not a deadline. That's a timestamp that's already meaningful in this framework. 22 minutes 22 seconds. 2222 central. The number carries weight before the song does.
